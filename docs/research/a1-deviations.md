# A1 deviations from `docs/SPEC.md` section 11 (Amendment 1)

Recorded by work package A1 (the one interface update of section 11.8). Each row: the spec section, what was built, and why. The architect folds these back into the spec.

"Deviation" = the artifact differs from the text. "Choice" = the text was silent or ambiguous and the smallest sound reading was taken. "Note" = built as written, but the next package needs to know.

The vectors encode every row below: a contract that replays `interfaces/vectors/transitions.json` implements them.

## Findings (the text is wrong or incomplete)

| # | Spec | Kind | What was done | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D1 | 11.6 "`T_ff = t_Z + W_z`", "Invariant 1 holds: in FROZEN the locker's refund is enabled on `[t_Z, T_ff)`" | **Deviation** | `T_ff = max(t_Z + W_z, T_co)`. `Swap::t_ff()` in `escrow-logic`, `deadlines().t_ff`, the `frozen_until` of the `Frozen` event on both chains. Rows `Z2/Z3/Z4 when t_Z + W_z < T_co` and the gates `boundary/T_ff/*`. | With the text's formula a freeze *shortens* the locker's window whenever it comes more than `W_z` before `T_co`: `W_z` may be 3,600 while `D_c` is at least 3,600 (Solana), 7,200 (Ethereum) and more at deeper tiers. Example, Ethereum defaults: notice at `t`, `D_c` = 10,800, freeze at `t + 10` with `W_z` = 3,600: the claim-without-ready would open at `t + 3,610` instead of `t + 10,800`, before `T_cut` (`t + 3,900`), so the claimer of a false notice could claim before the locker's client is even allowed to give up on the lock. With a deep tier (`d × 60 > W_z`) `T_ff` would also precede the earliest `set_ready`, leaving no instant at which Z2 can unfreeze. Both contradict 11.6 ("a malicious watcher … costs … the locker nothing", "push the opening … from `T_co` to `T_ff`"). Found by the model's property test, then stated as the Kani harness `freeze::a_freeze_never_shortens_the_lockers_window`; restoring the text's formula makes that harness fail (`KANI.md`). The algebra of 11.6 is unchanged: `T_co <= T_ff < T_co + W_z < T_final − W_c − 86,400`. **Neither chain has freeze yet, so neither shares the flaw; both must implement the maximum.** |
| D2 | 11.1 "added to `open_value[asset]` when the swap is opened", "the subtraction saturates at zero" | **Deviation** (gap) | The *add* is exact and checked. An open whose counted value, or whose new open value, does not fit the chain's amount type (`u64` on Solana, `uint128` on Ethereum) is refused: `OpenCapExceeded` with the switch on (it is above every possible cap), `MathOverflow` with it off. In direction B this makes `amount + deposit <= max` a precondition of `take` even with caps off. | With caps off the text checks nothing, and in direction B the whole `amount` is counted at `take` although only the deposit is escrowed, so the sum is not bounded by any token supply. A wrapping or saturating add would break "the open value equals the sum of the counted values of the open swaps" for good, and later exits would under-report. The same bound keeps the direction-B claim payout `amount − fee + Dp` inside the amount type. Kani: `switches::lock_and_take_obey_the_caps`, `switches::counted_flag_is_an_invariant`, `switches::sequences_of_8_open_value_is_the_sum_of_open_swaps`. Unreachable with real token supplies; the Solana program today computes that payout with `saturating_add` (`settle.rs`), which would underpay silently rather than fail in the same unreachable case. |
| D3 | 3.2 invariant 1, direction B NOTICED | Choice (hardening) | The direction-B refund rows gated by `T_cut` (B9c, B9h) are also closed at `T_co`: `now < min(T_cut, T_co, T_final)`. | The bounds give `T_cut <= T_co − 2M`, so nothing reachable changes and no vector changed. Without it invariant 1 held only for records that satisfy the deadline order; Kani's `windows::secret_calls_are_enabled_for_at_most_one_party` takes **every** record and found the overlap `[T_co, T_cut)` on a record with `T_cut > T_co`. Section 3.2 already asks for this kind of defence in depth against `T_final`. |
