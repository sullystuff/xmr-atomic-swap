# WP3 deviations from `docs/SPEC.md` and the frozen interface

Recorded by work package 3 (Ethereum contract). Each row: the spec section or frozen artifact, what was built, and why. Nothing under `interfaces/` or `docs/` was edited; the contract is built to the frozen interface and `check-abi.mjs` passes.

"Deviation" = the contract differs from the text. "Vector" = a row of the frozen vectors that cannot be reproduced on Ethereum as written. "Choice" = the text was silent or ambiguous. "Note" = built as written, but the next package or the architect needs to know.

## For the SDK (WP5) and the end-to-end harness (WP11)

- **Deploying.** `forge script script/DeployAnvil.s.sol --rpc-url http://127.0.0.1:8545 --broadcast --unlocked --sender <an anvil account>` deploys the libraries, the implementation, the proxy and a mock USDC (6 decimals, `mint`, `setBlacklisted`, `setPaused`), and writes `ethereum/deployments/anvil.json` (`chainId`, `proxy`, `implementation`, `usdc`, `owner`, `treasury`). It refuses every chain id but 31337. The bytecode of `XmrSwap` has link placeholders; deploying it by hand needs the two library addresses.
- **Amendment 1.** `lock(…, watcher)`, `take(…, payout)`, `fund(…, watcher)`: the taker passes **its own address** as the default payout (zero is `BadValue`) and zero for "no watcher". A fresh deployment is fail-closed: read `capsOf(token)` (`on`, `maxSwap`, `maxOpen`, `openValue`); headroom is `max(0, maxOpen - openValue)`, and in direction B a swap counts `amount + deposit`. Refusals: `SwapCapExceeded`, `OpenCapExceeded`, `WatcherNotAllowed`, `FreezeWindowOutOfRange`. `freeze(swapId)` is the named watcher's or the locker's, in NOTICED before `tClaimOpen`; afterwards `state == 10` and the claim opens at `T_ff = max(tFreeze + freezeSecs, tClaimOpen)` (the `frozenUntil` of `Frozen`), until which the locker may `refund` or `setReady`. In direction B the latest notice is `tFinal - claimDelay - freezeSecs - claimWindow - 86400`. Claim proceeds of a direction-B swap go to `getSwap().payout`; if parked they are under that address and anyone may call `pushOwed(token, payout)`. `flags & 32` = counted in the open value.
- **Every swap starts PENDING**, in both directions; the maker's `approve`/`fund` must carry the swap's `openHash` (read it from `getSwap`, or compute it with `openHashEthereum`).
- **Callers.** `claim` and `refund` are open to anyone. `setReady` is the locker's (A: taker wallet or session key; B: operator). `postNotice`, `abort` and `publishEvidence` are the claimer's (A: operator; B: taker wallet or session key). `cancelPending` is the taker wallet's or the session key's.
- **Check order and error names** are those of `transitions.json`: direction, caller, state and open hash, time, then secret or evidence. An unknown swap id is `BadState`.
- **Parked payouts.** Listen for `PayoutDeferred(token, account, amount)` and read `owedOf(token, account)`; the account itself calls `withdrawOwed(token, to)` and may name another destination. See row 18 for which account that is.
- **A pool share is consumed at `approve`**, by `keyAddr`. Two pending locks may name the same leaf; the second `approve` fails with `KeyAlreadyUsed` and the daemon rejects with reason 2. `lock` already refuses a leaf whose key is consumed.
- **Flags** (`Swap.flags`): 1 notice on record, 2 evidence on record, 4 a secret is revealed (`Swap.revealed`), 8 revealed by the locker (a refund), 16 late notice.
- **Tests and time.** With `via_ir` the optimizer may reuse an earlier `block.timestamp` after `vm.warp` inside one test function; test code should read the clock with `vm.getBlockTimestamp()`. Arguments of a pranked call that themselves make a call (a `sha256`, a view) must be computed before `vm.prank`.
- **Gas** (a whole transaction, `forge test --isolate --match-contract GasTest -vv`): the table is in `STORAGE.md`; `lock` is the expensive call.
