# WP5 (SDK) deviations from `docs/SPEC.md`

Recorded while building the unit part (milestone M1). Same vocabulary as
`docs/research/wp1-deviations.md`: **Deviation** = what was built differs from the text;
**Choice** = the text was silent or ambiguous and the smallest sound reading was taken;
**Note** = built as written, but the next package needs to know; **Request** = an interface
change this package would like from WP1 (nothing under `interfaces/`, `packages/protocol` or
`packages/core-wasm` was edited; the SDK builds to what is frozen).

Where the specification and a frozen artifact disagree, the artifact was followed: WP1's C7
(the Ethereum `quote_response` carries the compact form), E1 (`viewShare`), S1 (`Evidence`
struct), C15 (no `both` rows) and M1 to M12 (model conventions) are all taken as frozen.

## Choices in the flows (SPEC 3.11, 7.5 leave them open)

| # | Spec | Kind | What was done | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F1 | 3.11 taker (4) "at depth before `T_cut` call `set_ready`, at `T_cut` otherwise call `refund`" | Choice | If the lock is confirmed at depth after `T_cut` but the refund has not been sent yet (a tab that came back late), the flow sets ready (allowed until `T_co`) instead of refunding. Otherwise as written: refund from `T_cut`, never after `T_co − M`. | Both parties are better off, and the risk is the same as setting ready before `T_cut`. |
| F2 | 3.2 rule 2 | Choice | A secret-bearing call is proposed only while it stays acceptable for `M − 1` more seconds: taker refund until `T_co − M` (NOTICED) or `T_final − M`; maker claim until `T_cd − M` (READY) or `T_exp − M`; direction-B taker claim until `T_cd − M` (READY) or `T_exp − M` (the two claim windows `[T_co, T_final)` and `[T_final, T_exp)` are one continuous window); maker refund until `T_co − M` / `T_final − M`. Refunds in ACTIVE have no limit (valid at every instant). A unit test checks all of this against the model for every state in `transitions.json`. | The text names the boundaries; this is the arithmetic. |
| F3 | 3.4 A8 versus A10g | Choice | After `T_note` with no notice the taker's flow sends the keyless `refund_unnoticed`, not the keyed refund. | Same funds, no secret published. |
| F4 | 7.5 direction A step 5 | Choice | The maker relays its prepared lock only before `T_cut`; later it aborts. With no ready at `T_co` the default is: claim if its own lock verifies as confirmed, otherwise abort (`withoutReady` overrides). | After `T_cut` the taker's tab refunds by itself, so a late relay only burns fees. |
| F5 | 3.5 B9b | Choice | With no notice at `T_note` the maker's flow refunds at once (it is valid in ACTIVE and, if a late notice lands first, in NOTICED). | The escrow is the maker's capital; the text only says the refund exists. |
| F6 | 4.5 `close_swap` | Note | The flows propose `close_swap` (Solana) once the caller has swept and nothing is owed: the taker after a claim in A, the operator after a refund in A or a claim in B. It is the one proposed call the model does not know. | |
| F7 | 5.6 "clients keep history attached to both keys after an unlink" | Choice | `LinkMemory`: a client-side set of pairs that were ever seen mutually linked; `mergedReputation` keeps merging for a remembered pair. A reader with no memory sees only current state. | Current chain state alone cannot show a past link; the alternative is replaying `LinkChanged` events, which is a later addition. |
| F8 | 8.6 router | Choice | Effective rate = price × (1 + `dep_bps` × withhold rate); a maker with no history counts as withhold rate 1. The depth requirement is applied to each wave's total XMR and the wave is re-planned without the offers that fail it. Expected duration is `W_a` (if pending) + (depth + 1) × 120 s per wave, for display only. | "Price plus expected deposit cost" is not defined further. |
| F9 | 5.4 "sizes the budget for four calls at a capped gas price" | Choice | `ethSessionBudget(maxFeePerGas, gasPerCall = 150,000, calls = 4)`. The per-call gas is a placeholder until measured against WP3's contract. | |

## Integration (milestone M2): what running against the real program, contract and nodes showed

No bug was found in the program (WP2), the contract (WP3) or the facade (WP4): every refusal the
SDK predicted was the one they gave, by name, and every balance matched to the unit.

| # | Subject | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I1 | D10 byte order (Ethereum) | **Confirmed.** `bytes32` fields hold the same 32 bytes as on Solana; `revealed` read back from storage is the share as an integer, and `jointSpendKey` built from it sweeps real XMR in both directions. |
| I2 | D9 parked payouts (Ethereum) | **Confirmed, and the SDK was extended.** Parked payouts are keyed by account: the taker, the maker's payout address (a claim in A), its operator (a refund in B), or the maker's owner address (B11). New `EthereumClient.owedFor(token, taker, maker)` returns the four figures and the three maker-side addresses; `getSwap(…, withOwed)` now sums all three into `owedMaker`. **The flows say `withdraw_owed` with caller `owner`; on Ethereum the account that must send `withdrawOwed` is whichever of those addresses `owedFor` shows a balance for** (the test parks a claim by blacklisting the payout address; only the payout address can pull it). |
| I3 | Error decoding | **Confirmed.** Solana: `{ InstructionError: [i, { Custom: 6000 + n }] }` from `simulateTransaction` maps to the IDL names (`TooEarly`, `TooLate`, `ApprovalRequired`, `ApprovalInvalid`, `QuoteExpired`, `EvidenceMismatch`, `EvidenceRequired`). Ethereum: custom-error revert data from `eth_call` decodes by name (`TooEarly`, `TooLate`, `BadSecret`, `BadState`, `BadSigner`, `Cancelled`, `Revoked`, `WrongParty`, `ApprovalRequired`, `AmountOutOfRange`, `EvidenceMismatch`, `EvidenceRequired`, `Unauthorized`, `NothingOwed`). Because the SDK simulates first, none of these refused calls was ever broadcast; a refused `claim` left no secret on chain. |
| I4 | Account closing (Solana) | **Confirmed.** A "closed" row (`refund_unnoticed`) removes the swap account in the same instruction and `SwapWatcher` reports it once with `swap: null`. `close_swap` by the session key (after a claim in A) and by the operator (after a refund in A, after a claim in B) returns all rent to the taker and sweeps a signing session key to zero. On Ethereum the same rows end in DONE. |
| I5 | Reading before the deployment block (Ethereum) | **SDK fix.** A view call at a block tag that predates the contract (on a fresh chain `finalized` is block 0) answers `0x`; viem then threw a decoding error. `read` now throws `ContractError('NoContract')` and `getSwap` returns `null` there. |
| I6 | Finality (Ethereum) | anvil's `finalized` tag is 64 blocks behind the head. `getSwap` at the default tag shows nothing until then, `isFinal(txHash)` turns true exactly then, and `EthEventWatcher` (which follows `finalized`) reports each event once, in order, only after that. |
| I7 | Session key (Solana) | The session key pays its own fees from the budget (`rent minimum + 2,000,000` lamports); a `refund` or `claim` it signs sweeps the rest to the taker, so the key reads 0 afterwards. A lock with an advance approval pays for two signatures. Both asserted to the lamport. |
| I8 | Offers go stale | A direction-A lock needs the offer refreshed within `valid_secs`; `offerProblem` reports `OfferStale` and the program refuses the lock with the same name. |
| I9 | SDK additions made for integration | `solanaInstruction` / `ethereumCall` (`src/flows/execute.ts`: a flow decision to the instruction or calldata, refusing a secret or evidence that would not open); `SolanaClient.sendAndConfirm` / `waitConfirmed` (polling; no WebSocket); `EthereumClient.waitMined`; `owedFor` (I2). |
| I10 | Not exercised | The v0 lookup-table transaction (P4) and a real duplicate one-time key on regtest (WP1 C13) remain untested against a runtime; direction-B refund rows and the 60-day split are covered by the unit tests against the model only. |
| I11 | Sweeps on a thin regtest chain | `sweepJoint` failed intermittently with `SweepDaemon: … "hit decoy selection round limit"`. Cause: monero-wallet 0.2.0 draws decoy ages from wallet2's gamma distribution (centred near two days) with a budget of 160 draws for 15 decoys; on a chain of about 180 blocks most draws fall before the start of the chain and many of the rest hit coinbase outputs inside their 60-block lock. Measured: 3 of 40 dry-run sweeps fail at height 180, 0 of 40 at 600. Fix in the harness (`test/integration/xmr.ts`): every file mines to `MIN_CHAIN_HEIGHT = 720` before anything else and `sweep` refuses a shorter chain. Not a production condition (mainnet has over 10^8 RingCT outputs). `tests/monero-regtest/lib/harness.mjs` (`ensureFunded(160)`) has the same exposure for anything that sweeps through swap-core at that height; it was not changed here. |

## Explicit requests in the flows (BUG-1 of `tests/e2e/BUGS.md`)

| # | Subject | What was done |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | A confirmed user request was dropped where an automatic branch returned first | Rule now applied everywhere: an explicit request wins over every automatic step while the contract still accepts the call that carries it out (within the broadcast margin for a secret); when it no longer can, the decision carries `refused: <reason>` (new optional field on every `Decision`, type `Refusal`) and otherwise says what happens instead. |
| R2 | Taker, direction A, `cancel` | NOTICED: refund (A10b) at any time up to `T_co − M`, whatever is known about the lock and before any fact is asked for (this was BUG-1: a confirmed lock led to "wait for the ready minimum", then `set_ready`). After `T_co − M`: `refund-window-closed`. READY before `T_cd`: `ready-already-set`. After `T_final − M` (READY, ABORTED): `refund-window-closed` until `T_exp`. After the maker's claim: `already-settled`. |
| R3 | Taker, direction B, `cancel` | ACTIVE with nothing paid: the address is never shown; `cannot-exit-after-funding` (the taker has no call there; the maker refunds or the deposit returns at `T_exp`). This was the same defect: the request was ignored and the address shown. NOTICED and READY: `abort` (B8) wins over waiting and over the claim until `T_final`; from `T_final`: `abort-window-closed` and the claim proceeds. |
| R4 | Maker | An explicit reject no longer waits for the taker's DLEQ proof; where a take cannot be rejected (Solana, outside window mode): `reject-not-available`. `backOut` in direction B now also wins in NOTICED over setting ready (B9c/B9h before `T_cut`, B9d after) up to `T_co − M`, then `refund-window-closed`; in READY before `T_cd`: `ready-already-set`. `withhold` at or after `T_hold`: `hold-ended`. `withhold` or `release` with no deposit in the hold: `no-deposit-held`. `withoutReady: 'abort'` from `T_final`: `abort-window-closed` (the claim proceeds). |
