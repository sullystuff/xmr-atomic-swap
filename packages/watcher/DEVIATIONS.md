# @xmrswap/watcher: deviations, choices and findings (work package A5)

Only files under `packages/watcher/` were created or edited. `pnpm-workspace.yaml` already lists `packages/*`, so the package joined the workspace with no root change; `pnpm install --no-frozen-lockfile` updated `pnpm-lock.yaml`, which is not committed by this package.

Kinds: **Gap** (the spec or the SDK lacks something; a proposal follows), **Choice** (the spec is silent; the smallest sound reading), **Note**.

## Watch requests and the API

| # | Spec | Kind | What was done | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| W-A1 | 11.6 `POST /watch/v1/swaps`, "`{ chain, swap_ref, notice_key }`" | **Gap (for SPEC)** | A watch request carries nothing that proves it comes from the locker. The service authenticates what it can: the key must be sealed to this watcher for this chain and swap (SDK W2), and the swap must exist at finality, name this watcher and be open. It cannot check the notice key itself before a notice exists (the key is an HKDF output of a Diffie-Hellman secret it never sees), and once a notice exists it cannot tell "wrong key, good notice" from "right key, false notice". So: it keeps up to `max_keys_per_swap` (default 4) **different candidate keys** per swap, uses the one that opens the notice and deletes the rest. A wrong key posted before or after the locker's own cannot cause a freeze (test `api.test.ts`, both orders). **Residual:** if a stranger posts a wrong key and the locker never registers, the notice does not open and the swap is frozen at once, as 2.8 prescribes for "does not open" (test `safety.test.ts`, "a notice key the watcher cannot check"). The cost is one freeze fee to the watcher and a delay of at most `W_z` to the claimer; the returning locker undoes it with `set_ready`; no funds are at risk. A stranger can also fill the candidate slots first, in which case the locker's own request is refused with `too_many_keys` and its UI must treat the swap as not watched. **Proposed fix:** make the seal authenticated by the locker's on-chain encryption key: `k = HKDF(X25519(eph, W) ‖ X25519(locker_enc_secret, W), …)` with `locker_enc_pub` read from the swap (`taker_enc_key` in A, `maker_enc_key` in B). The watcher can then verify the sender from chain state, and one key per swap suffices. This needs a change to `sealNoticeKey` / `openSealedNoticeKey` in the SDK and a line in 11.6. | The task requires that a malicious request cannot make the service freeze a swap whose lock is good. With the request format as frozen by the SDK that holds whenever the locker's own key is on file, and not otherwise. |
| W-A2 | 11.6 routes (three) | Choice | A fourth route, `GET /watch/v1/health`: endpoint reachability, chain time, fee balance, reserve per freeze, runway, today's spend, Monero nodes answering and agreeing. Cached five seconds. `info` gains `chains` (which chains this instance serves); the SDK's `parseWatchInfo` ignores it. | The task asks for a health/status route. Balances and addresses are public on chain. |
| W-A3 | 11.6 `GET /watch/v1/swaps/<swap_ref>`: "what it has seen and done" | Choice | The route is unauthenticated (the SDK's `WatcherClient.status` sends nothing but the reference, which is public). It answers with the watch's state, the reason, what it is waiting for, the decision and the ids of the freeze transactions the watcher sent. It never includes the amount, a party, the Monero transaction, an address or a key. An unknown and a malformed reference get the same 404. No route enumerates watches. | "An unauthenticated party must not … enumerate watches, or learn anything about other watches." Knowing a reference, one learns that the swap is watched here and how far the watch has come (`waiting_for: recheck` implies the lock verified). That much is the route's purpose. |
| W-A4 | 11.6 "`watching_until: T_co`" | Choice | Once a notice is on record: the swap's own `T_co`. Before: the latest it can have, `T_note + D_c` (as SDK W3). | No `T_co` exists before the notice. |
| W-A5 | 11.6 "Accepted only if the swap exists at `finalized`, names this watcher and is not settled" | Choice | Also refused: a swap that is READY or FROZEN (`swap_ended`), one whose `T_co − M` is past or closer than `margins.min_lead_secs` (default 120 s) (`too_late`), a direction-B swap still PENDING (no watcher is named before `fund`: `not_watchable`), and a chain this instance has no endpoint for. | The service would acknowledge a watch it cannot perform. |
| W-A6 | 11.6 "a cap on watched swaps, rate limits" | Choice | `max_swaps` (default 200) → `full`. Per client IP per minute: 60 requests, 10 watch requests. Body cap 1,024 bytes → 413. | Sizes are not given. |

## The loop

| # | Spec | Kind | What was done | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| W-A7 | 2.11 "Fail closed: any disagreement or unreachable node means not confirmed"; 11.6 "freeze … when the lock is not `confirmed` at `T_cut`" | Note | **Two Monero nodes that disagree, or one that does not answer, mean "not confirmed": nothing happens before `T_cut`, and the swap is frozen at `T_cut`.** After `T_cut` a lock that loses agreement is frozen while the window is open. This is the SDK's `watcherFacts` + `watcherStep`, unchanged. | Failing closed means failing toward the action that protects the locker's funds. A freeze costs at most `W_z` of delay and the locker undoes it with `set_ready`; an unfrozen false lock is a total loss. The price: a watcher with broken Monero nodes freezes every swap that reaches `T_cut` (README, "It can freeze wrongly"). |
| W-A8 | 11.6 "stop watching when the swap is READY, FROZEN or settled" | Choice | A watch is closed, and its notice key deleted, only after the same ending was read `stop_confirmations` passes in a row (default 3). | One wrong or lagging answer from an endpoint must not end a watch. An honest chain never un-ends a swap at finality, so this costs a few seconds. |
| W-A9 | 11.6 "exists at `finalized`"; SDK `WatcherFacts.now`: "chain time of that read" | Choice | Swap state and the clock are both read at `finalized` (settings `solana.commitment`, `ethereum.read_tag` allow less). On Ethereum the finalized clock runs about 13 minutes behind the head, so a freeze goes out that much after `T_cut` and the service's own "too late" trips that much after the true `T_co − M`. Both are safe: `T_cut <= T_co − 2M` leaves at least `M` = 30 minutes, and the freeze is simulated at `latest` before it is signed. | One consistent snapshot per pass, as the SDK's flows assume. |
| W-A10 | 11.6 terminal states | Choice | Three terminal records: `frozen` (FROZEN on chain and this watcher sent a freeze), `not_needed` (ready; ended; frozen by the locker; or the lock stayed confirmed until `T_co − M`), `window_missed` (a freeze was due or the swap could not be judged, and nothing landed; the reason is recorded). A freeze decision journaled before a crash is dropped if, after the restart and before any transaction was built, the step no longer asks for it. | "Sends `freeze` when and only when it says so." |
| W-A11 | not in the spec | Choice | Retry: any failure in a pass is retried with backoff 2 s, 4 s, … capped at 60 s, per swap, while the window is open. A broadcast transaction not seen after `margins.attempt_timeout_secs` (150 s): on Solana it is dead (its blockhash expired) and a fresh one is built; on Ethereum it is replaced at the **same nonce** with both fee fields raised by 12.5%, so at most one is mined. | |
| W-A12 | 11.6 "one Solana key and one Ethereum key with a small fee balance" | Choice | Fees: per-swap and per-UTC-day limits per chain, counted on the maximum each journaled transaction can cost (Ethereum transactions sharing a nonce count once). A watch reserves one worst-case freeze; a new watch is refused (`insufficient_fee_balance`) when the balance is below `(open watches + 1) × reserve`, or when the per-swap limit is below one reserve. | The task's fee handling. |

## The SDK

| # | Where | Kind | Finding |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S1 | `EthereumClient.send` | **Gap** | It takes the chain id from `eth_chainId` and the gas limit from `eth_estimateGas × 1.2` of the same endpoint, with no bound, and its fee cap is optional. That is right for a wallet-driven client and wrong for an unattended hot key: a dishonest endpoint chooses what chain the signature is valid on and how much gas it may burn. Reproduction: `test/safety.test.ts`, "the SDK signs for whatever chain id and gas the endpoint reports". The watcher therefore does not call `send` (nor `SolanaClient.prepare`, whose compute-unit limit follows the endpoint's simulation up to 1.4 M). It builds the freeze with the SDK's builders (`XmrSwapProgram.freeze`, `XmrSwapContract.freeze`, `legacyTransaction`, `cuLimitFromSimulation`), pins the chain id, uses a fixed gas limit of `ETH_WATCHER_GAS.freeze × 1.25` = 98,250, bounds the fee, and signs through a guard that accepts nothing but a freeze. **Requested:** optional `chainId` and `gasLimit` pins in `EthereumClientOptions`. |
| S2 | `SolanaTransport` | Gap | No `getBalance` and no block height. The balance is read as `getAccount(key).lamports`; blockhash expiry is judged by wall-clock age (150 s, well past the roughly 60 to 90 s a finalized blockhash stays valid). |
| S3 | `@xmrswap/sdk/testing` `FakeEthereum` | Gap | No `eth_getBalance`. The unit tests wrap the fake's transport. |
| S4 | SDK integration harness | Note | `packages/sdk/test/integration/{sol,eth,xmr,global-setup}.ts` are not exported. The watcher's real-chain tests import them, and the SDK sources they are typed against, by relative path, unchanged. The service under test always imports the built `@xmrswap/sdk`. |
| S5 | `watcherFacts` | Note | A notice key that does not open the notice and a notice that is false are the same answer (`noticeOpens: false`), by design. W-A1 is the consequence at the service boundary. |
